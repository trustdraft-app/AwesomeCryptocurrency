# GridLens 360 — A Simple Guide for Leadership

*Real-Time Grid Intelligence, in your pocket. No training needed — this page is all you'll ever need.*

---

## Getting the app on your iPhone (about 2 minutes, one time)

You will receive an **email invitation** from Apple TestFlight. Then:

1. **Install the free “TestFlight” app** from the App Store (search: *TestFlight*). You only ever do this once.
2. **Open the invitation email on your iPhone** and tap **“View in TestFlight”** (or **Accept**).
3. Tap **Install**. GridLens 360 appears on your home screen like any normal app.
4. Tap it to open. That’s it — you’re in.

> There is no login, no password, and no setup. It just opens.

---

## What you’re looking at

At the **bottom of the screen** there are **five buttons**. Each one answers a single question at a glance. Tap between them freely — nothing can break.

| Button | Tap it when you want to know… |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| 🟦 **Command** | *“How is the whole Kingdom right now?”* — total demand, how close we are to the all-time record, spare capacity, and a live map of every region. **Tap any region on the map** to see its number. |
| 🟧 **Peak** | *“What was today’s peak in the Eastern region, and is the system stable?”* — today vs. yesterday demand curve, frequency and balance dials, area loads. |
| 🟩 **Power** | *“Do we have enough generation and reserve?”* — the generation mix, reserves, and how loaded the inter-region links are. |
| ☀️ **Clean** | *“How much clean energy is flowing?”* — solar & wind now, and the battery fleet. Use the toggle at the top to switch **Renewables ↔ Storage**. |
| 🛡️ **Assets** | *“Which substations are close to their safe limit?”* — colour-coded risk (green = fine, amber = watch, red = tight). |

---

## Three things worth knowing

- **The amber “SNAPSHOT” badge** (top-right) means the figures are a **fixed, realistic demo snapshot** taken at one moment (18:00). It is deliberately *not* live yet — so you can review the app safely anywhere, with no network needed. When it goes live, that badge will change.
- **Colours tell the story fast:** green is healthy, amber means keep an eye on it, red means little headroom. Every colour also has a small symbol (✓ ! ▲) so it reads clearly for everyone.
- **You cannot break anything.** Tap, swipe, and explore. There are no destructive buttons — it is a read-only view of the grid.

---

## If something looks off

- Close the app fully and reopen it (swipe up from the bottom, flick GridLens 360 away, tap it again).
- Still odd? Reply to your TestFlight invitation email, or contact your administrator — the app updates silently, and a fresh version may already be waiting under the **TestFlight** app’s **Update** button.

*That’s everything. Open it, glance, decide.*
